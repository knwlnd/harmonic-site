Feedback

Specific observations help shape the alpha.

The preferred feedback and bug-reporting path will be a dedicated form. Until that URL is confirmed, this page documents the information testers should prepare.

Feedback form

The alpha feedback form link will be added here once the form is configured. Do not submit confidential project material unless explicitly requested.

Common details

Name, email, Harmonic version, macOS version, Mac model, DAW or host, virtual instruments in use, feedback type, summary, detailed description and whether follow-up contact is allowed.

Bug reports

Include what you were trying to do, what happened, what you expected, whether it reproduces, reproduction steps, whether Harmonic crashed and whether the issue blocked testing.

Experience reports

Describe what you were creating, what felt natural, what interrupted your flow, where you used the mouse, what took longer than expected and what would make you return to Harmonic.

Compatibility reports

Include the DAW or host, virtual instruments, routing setup, MIDI destinations, audio interface if relevant and the behaviour you observed.
