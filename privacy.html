Privacy

Simple alpha privacy statement.

This website does not require an account. Harmonic is intended to operate locally and does not currently require a cloud service.

Website

The public website is static and does not require user accounts. It may link to third-party services used for alpha applications or feedback.

Alpha forms

Third-party services may collect submitted contact, device, compatibility and diagnostic information. Those submissions are used for product development and alpha support.

Musical content

No musical content should be collected unless a tester explicitly attaches it. Testers should avoid submitting confidential project material.

Attribution

Tester names, quotes, music, screenshots or testimonials will not be used publicly without explicit permission.
