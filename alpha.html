Private alpha

Testing with musicians while the interaction model is refined.

Harmonic is currently being tested with a small group of musicians while its core interaction model, compatibility and documentation are refined.

Who the alpha is for

Early testers should use a Mac, own one or more MIDI-capable virtual instruments, be comfortable configuring basic MIDI routing, be willing to use unfinished software and provide specific observations from real musical workflows.

What testers receive

Approved testers may receive selected alpha builds, setup documentation, release notes, known-issues information and a feedback route. Access is controlled while distribution and onboarding are evaluated.

What to expect

Expect unfinished features, occasional bugs, changing workflows, manual build distribution, limited support capacity and no guarantee of project-file compatibility between early builds.

Useful feedback

The most useful feedback explains what you were trying to create, what interrupted your flow, which instruments and host were involved, where MIDI routing was unclear and whether Harmonic helped you use instruments that were previously underused.

Alpha access

There is no unrestricted public download at this stage. Musicians interested in testing Harmonic can submit the alpha intake form for review.
