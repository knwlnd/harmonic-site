Roadmap

Capability areas, not a public promise list.

The roadmap is guided by a simple question: does this capability help a musician remain in performance and reach a musical result more naturally?

Established

Foundation

Keyboard-first performance model, deterministic musical engine, multi-output MIDI architecture, tonal-system foundation, independent Chords, Bass and Solo routing, continuous interface feedback, refined core architecture and automated test coverage.

Current focus

Alpha expansion

Additional tonal systems, harmonic minor and related modes, performance refinements, expanded musical behaviour, compatibility testing, alpha onboarding, documentation, workflow refinement, stability improvements and release packaging.

Exploration

Future directions

Expressive bass interpretation, optional pad or orchestration output, richer performance variation, saved configurations and presets, expanded controller support, broader orchestration workflows, improved capture and export and public distribution.
